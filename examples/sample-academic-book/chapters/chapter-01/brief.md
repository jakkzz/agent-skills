# Chapter 1: Why Evidence Traceability Matters — Brief

## Purpose

Introduce evidence traceability and explain why the rest of the book uses explicit source, claim, review, and approval records.

## Reader starting point

The reader understands ordinary citations but may not distinguish discovery records, abstracts, and full-text evidence.

## Central lesson

A citation is most useful when the reader and author can recover the exact evidence and decision it supports.

## Required topics

- Evidence levels
- Atomic claims
- Human approval gates

## Excluded topics

Detailed systematic-review methodology is deferred to a later chapter.

## Target length

A short demonstration section for the fixture.
