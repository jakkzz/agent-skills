# Why Evidence Traceability Matters — Draft V1

## 1.1 From citation to traceability

A bibliography establishes source identity, but identity alone does not establish what a source supports. Evidence traceability connects an atomic claim to a reviewed excerpt or paraphrase and an exact locator [@Smith2025]. This distinction prevents a search-result title, citation count, or plausible abstract from silently becoming stronger evidence than the author actually read.

## 1.2 Human ownership

The author owns the thesis, interpretation, source-selection judgment, voice, ethics, and final decisions. An agent can search, organize, draft, and critique, but it cannot approve its own output. Artifact hashes make this boundary visible: editing an approved file invalidates its earlier approval.
