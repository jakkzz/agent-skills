# Evidence-Grounded Academic Writing — Style Guide

## Voice

Direct, measured, and instructional. Use first-person plural only for genuine author decisions.

## Tone and formality

Academic but approachable. Define terms before using them as abstractions.

## Citation density

Attach citations to the exact claim they support. Do not place one citation at the end of a paragraph containing several unrelated claims.

## Non-negotiable rules

- Never invent citations, quotations, evidence, statistics, or page numbers.
- Distinguish evidence from interpretation and author expertise.
- Preserve uncertainty and disagreement.
