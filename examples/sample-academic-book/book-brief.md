# Evidence-Grounded Academic Writing — Book Brief

## Field

Higher education and scholarly communication.

## Intended readers

Graduate students and university instructors beginning long-form academic writing.

## Central thesis

Academic writing is more trustworthy when claims, source evidence, author decisions, and revisions remain traceable across the entire book lifecycle.

## Reader outcomes

- Distinguish discovery metadata from claim-support evidence.
- Maintain reviewable chapter artifacts and human approval gates.
- Recognize where automation must stop for scholarly judgment.

## Scope boundaries

This sample demonstrates workflow structure, not a substantive literature review.
